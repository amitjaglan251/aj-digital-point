## Safe data-quality actions
- Do not infer vacancy counts, fees, eligibility, or selection stages from the listing title.
- Keep official notice link and application link distinct; a “View” link to an advertisement is not automatically an online application portal.
- Re-check short-deadline records before labelling them active/expired; DRDO listing metadata can change.
- Results, selected-candidate lists and interview outcomes must not be displayed as open recruitment forms.
- This batch does not certify any of the 371 records as fully verified. A record is “fully verified” only after the official notice supports its title/post name, advertisement number, dates, vacancies, eligibility, fee, selection method and application route, or each unavailable field is explicitly labelled “not stated in notice”.
