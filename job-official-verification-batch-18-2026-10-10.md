## Notice-verified fields
- Post: Junior Research Fellow (JRF).
- Advertisement number: DMRL/HRD/JRF/2026/01.
- Laboratory/location: Defence Metallurgical Research Laboratory (DMRL), Hyderabad.
- Total advertised positions: 18, subject to the lab's stated discretion and candidate suitability.
- Discipline-wise positions: Metallurgical Engineering / Materials Science — 7; Mechanical Engineering — 7; Chemical Engineering — 2; Physics — 2.
- Engineering routes: First-class relevant B.E./B.Tech with valid GATE score, or first-class relevant M.E./M.Tech with first-class relevant B.E./B.Tech, as specified for each discipline.
- Physics route: M.Sc. Physics, First Division, with valid NET qualification.
- Fellowship: ₹37,000 per month plus HRA as per DRDO rules.
- Age: maximum 28 years on the closing date of interview; relaxation stated as 5 years for SC/ST and 3 years for OBC, subject to the notice/Government rules.
- Tenure: initially two years, potentially extendable by two further years subject to review; notice describes a maximum four-year tenure and possible progression to SRF after assessment.
- Application process: download and complete the Excel application form, then email it with the required supporting documents to dmrlhrd@gmail.com. The notice asks for the filled spreadsheet plus a single PDF containing supporting certificates/documents.
- Shortlisting: valid GATE/NET score and/or qualifying marks, according to the candidate's degree route and discipline; interview in Hyderabad on a date to be communicated later.
- The notice says applications close 21 days after publication on the DRDO website. The DRDO listing says 06/10/2026 start and 27/10/2026 end, consistent with the 21-day rule.
- Fee: the reviewed notice does not specify an application fee. Do not invent a fee or label ₹0 unless the source explicitly supports it.
- Not an employment guarantee: the notice states that the fellowship does not confer a right to absorption into DRDO.
